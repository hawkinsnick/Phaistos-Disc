# Corpus Project AI Research Contract — 0.3
The repository corpus is authoritative; the AI layer is rebuildable, not a second corpus.
## Evidence
Separate observation/source record, normalization/derivation, computational result, scholarly interpretation, and AI-derived analysis. Missing evidence is unknown or blocked.
## Provenance and citation
Prefer stable IDs, repository paths and source locators. Preserve hashes and lineage. Copies of one witness are not independent witnesses.
## Rights
Consult indexed rights/NOTICE artifacts. Transformation does not broaden upstream rights.
## Cross-corpus safety
Interoperability permits comparison, not equivalence. Similarity alone does not establish values, relationship, borrowing, morphology, decipherment, or replication.
## Staleness
Read generated/source-state.json; if stale, use canonical files and report the mismatch.
