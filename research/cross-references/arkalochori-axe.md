# Arkalochori bronze axe

Canonical object record: [CRET-DISPUTED-HM-X2416](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Cretan-hieroglpyhs/blob/main/research/disputed-inscriptions/arkalochori-axe.json).

The bronze axe has a disputed script assignment. Comparisons with Linear A, Cretan Hieroglyphic and the Phaistos Disc remain attributed hypotheses. This cross-reference adds no transcribed occurrences or analytically admitted records to this corpus. The small gold and silver Linear A axes from the same cave are separate objects.

Family register: [Eteocypriot 1.0 disputed objects](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Eteocypriot/blob/v1.0.0/research/disputed-cretan-objects.json).
